# Prompt gambar — Bolu Dadang (Nano Banana / Gemini)

Catatan: gambar AI hanya untuk mockup, tekstur, dan OG image. Foto menu/produk asli tetap harus foto asli sebelum publikasi. Semua output Nano Banana membawa watermark SynthID.

Alur konsisten:
1. Generate **MASTER** dulu, simpan sebagai `master-original.png`.
2. Untuk varian lain, unggah master sebagai **reference image** dan pakai prompt varian + kalimat "keep the same composition, camera angle, lighting, background and props; change only the crumb colour/pattern".
3. Jangan minta teks/logo di dalam gambar.
4. Rasio: produk 4:5, hero 16:9, galeri 1:1 atau 4:5, tekstur 3:2.

## MASTER — Original

> Photorealistic food photograph of Indonesian bolu kelemben, a rectangular Banjar sponge cake about 20 cm long, golden-brown top with a fine cracked crust, pale butter-yellow crumb with visible airy pores, cut into thick rectangular slices, one slice pulled slightly forward to show the crumb. On a worn wooden board over warm cream linen. Soft window light from the left, gentle natural shadows, 50mm lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field, warm homemade atmosphere. No frosting, no icing, no toppings, no cream, no text, no logo, no hands. 4:5.

## Tekstur (section Tentang)

> Macro photograph of bolu kelemben sponge texture, warm golden crumb with fine irregular air pockets, soft directional light revealing depth, edge of the crust at the top, minimal composition, no text, no logo. 3:2.

## OG image

> Editorial 16:9 food photograph of bolu kelemben slices on a wooden board with banana leaf and a linen napkin, warm cream background, soft natural light, composition weighted to the right with clean negative space on the left for a title, no text, no logo, no hands. 16:9.

## Negative prompt (tempel di akhir semua prompt)

> Avoid: frosting, buttercream, icing, whipped cream, sprinkles, fruit toppings, candles, cake stand with dome, plastic studio look, HDR, oversaturated colours, floating crumbs, duplicate slices, deformed shapes, English text, Indonesian text, logos, watermarks, hands, fingers, people.

## Checklist hasil

- Bentuknya bolu kelemben (sponge padat-lembut, potongan persegi), bukan cake ulang tahun.
- Tidak ada krim/frosting.
- Warna varian masuk akal: pandan hijau pucat alami, coklat coklat tua, zebra jelas.
- Tekstur crumb terlihat, bukan permukaan plastik.
- Tidak ada teks/logo; watermark SynthID tetap ada.
- Background dan cahaya konsisten antar varian.
